turning in eve crucible
this is different from Dr. Santornie's obeservations/calcualtions.
im not sure how, as he was using Dominion ~2009/2010.
the most notible of this was turning, in which ship would hit the min turn speed ~.71s into the turn (within first tic)
in crucible, the ship had to slow down to that min turn speed while turning a minor amount, then do major turn amount before resuming
accel and completing turn.
bezier curves define this perfectly, but i never got apex right.
apex->end should be same dir as start->dir


dead stop -> turn
ship at 0ms.  begin heading in desired direction


movement -> turn
turn amounts and how ship reacts...
depending on turn angle, ship will:
1)  slow while commencing slight direction change (~20%)
2)  major direction change at point of lowest speed (~60% of turn?  maybe 50%)
3)  resume accel to prev speed while continuing turn until completion (last 20% or so)

speed and ship variables will vary greatly between ships, pilots, and fits.
speed fraction (ASF) will NOT.

min speed for turn angle of x
sqrt(cos(x)+1 /2)

30*  .965
60*  .866
90*  .71
120* .50
150* .27
180* .0000001  (3.1811927190972009232900311292247e-47)
we'll call this a full stop for a complete 180* change


outline:
turn init will
 - determine degree of change
 - determine target asf for turn
 - set turn vars
 - calculate entire turn and asf and save in map
 - return to caller (MoveObject())
    turn to begin on next tic?
turn() will
 - set ship position and asf from turn map according to tic
 - set heading to next position (in case of vector change)
